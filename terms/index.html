1. What Tabs is — and what it isn't

Tabs is a personal tool for your time out: it helps you plan, keep track of drinks and spend, get a ride home, keep track of who owes who, and look back afterwards.

  • The intoxication estimate is a rough, educational range, not a measurement. It can't tell you whether you are safe or legal to drive, and it will never say so. Never drive after drinking. Get a ride.
  • Tabs is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical condition, and nothing in it is medical advice. If you're worried about your drinking or your health, speak to a healthcare professional.
  • Tabs' safety tools help you look after yourself. They are not a safety or monitoring service. A "check on me" reminder, or its follow-up, can come late (battery saving can delay it by many minutes) or not at all (notifications off, or the phone off or flat). Tabs never contacts anyone for you: it opens a message, and nothing goes until you tap Send. Your location can be approximate. Rides are provided by Uber and Bolt, not by us, and may not be available. Tell someone where you're going.
  • In an emergency, call 10177 (ambulance), 10111 (police) or 112 — don't rely on the app.

2. You must be 18 or older

Tabs is only for people aged 18 and over, and you may not use Tabs or buy Tabs+ if you are younger. By using Tabs you confirm that you are 18 or older.

If Tabs asks whether you're under 18 and you say yes, the app locks. It goes back to the 18+ check, crew sharing stops, and Tabs points you to Google Play to cancel Tabs+.

If you're under 18 and have bought the monthly Tabs+ plan, cancel it in Google Play (a yearly plan doesn't renew by itself). Tabs is free to use. Tabs+ is an optional subscription that adds the parts that learn your body (your sweet spot, "What if…?", tomorrow's forecast, and your nights read after the first one), your month in depth and the crew ledger. Your safety tools, including the live estimate, are always free.

A yearly plan never renews by itself: another year is a new purchase in Google Play, at the price it shows you. If you choose the monthly plan in the reminder, it starts when the year ends, so you never pay twice for the same time. If you choose to let it end, or choose nothing, your yearly plan ends at the end of the year, and you can pick the monthly plan at any time after that.
