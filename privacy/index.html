Tabs — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026 · Policy version: 2026-11-20.1
App: Tabs, a personal drinking co-pilot for South Africa
Package: za.co.tabsapp.tabs (Android)

Tabs is built privacy-first. Almost everything stays on your phone. Your drink log, your estimate and your body details never leave it, unless you send them somewhere yourself, for example in an export of your data. Information only goes anywhere when you use one of a few features, all listed below: sharing a round with your crew, buying Tabs+, pinning your home, getting a ride or walking directions to your car, or sending something to a friend.

This policy explains, in plain terms, what Tabs keeps, what leaves your phone and why, who else is involved, and your rights under the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

Who is responsible for your information

The Responsible Party is KrazyBuilds (Pty) Ltd (registration 2026/784933/07), 961 Steinkopf Street, Saron, Western Cape, 6812. The Information Officer is Keagan Christians, hello@krazybuilds.com. Email that address with any question, or to use any right below.

You must be 18 or older

Tabs is for adults. There's an 18+ age gate on first launch, with no bypass: if the birthday you confirm is under 18, the gate stays shut on that phone for 24 hours. Tabs keeps only the time of that check, never the birthday, and removes it the next time Tabs opens after the 24 hours. Tabs is not directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If Tabs asks whether you're under 18 and you say yes, the app locks and goes back to that gate, and crew sharing is switched off, which takes what you shared off our server (see "Withdrawing" under "Sharing a round with your crew" below). The same 24 hours apply there: after a birthday under 18 confirmed to unlock it, and after you delete everything from the locked app.

What Tabs keeps on your phone only

These are created and kept in the app's private storage on your phone. They are not sent to us or to anyone else, except in the specific cases in the next section:

  • Your sessions: when you went out and came home, the drinks you logged, your plan (drinks and budget), your spend, and whether you ate before.
  • The intoxication estimate and your reads: an educational range calculated on your phone from your drinks and body details, plus the "your nights, understood" and money reads. It is never a "safe to drive" signal and never leaves your phone.
  • Your profile and body details: your name, if you add one; your age in years, worked out from the birthday you give at the 18+ gate, which is required (Tabs keeps the age, not the birthday); and your optional weight, body type and height. Your age and body details are used only to make the estimate more personal. Body details never leave your phone.
  • Check-ins: how you felt, how you slept, your energy.
  • Your drinks: the drinks you save as yours, and your usual if you pick one.
  • Rounds and the crew ledger: who paid a round, what each person owes, and who has squared up. This includes the names you type for your friends, your PayShap number if you add one, and, only if you save them, a friend's phone number, whether to remind them by SMS or WhatsApp, and their PayShap number or handle. Friends' numbers and PayShap handles stay on your phone: they're never synced to the crew service and never sent to us. Tabs is ledger-only: it never holds, moves or processes money and stores no card or bank details.
  • Safety contacts: the names and phone numbers of the people you choose to be your go-to contacts.
  • Home and car: your saved home (an address, or a pin) and where you parked.
  • Tabs+ free days: when you switched on each of your free days, and which night's read was your free one.
  • The Pause, taste journal, Bounce back: the words you set for yourself, the drinks you rate, and the recovery steps you tick.
  • Reminders: the ones you set yourself (a "check on me", your Pause note, a ride reminder, a check-in reminder and the test buzz), and the ones Tabs sets for you (the follow-up when a "check on me" goes unanswered, and the reminder before a yearly Tabs+ plan ends). These are scheduled by your phone itself and are not sent anywhere.
  • Your consent choices and disclaimer acknowledgements: logged so we can honour them, and so every consent can be withdrawn (POPIA requires this). The version of our Terms of Service you agreed to, and when, is logged the same way, and so is the version of this policy when you see a change to it in the app.

Backups are off. Tabs switches off Android's automatic backup, so none of this is copied to Google Drive or to a new phone. When you remove the app, it's gone.

When you share something, you choose the recipient. An export of your data, your month's card, a money reminder, or your location goes through your phone's share sheet or messaging app, to the person or app you pick. A money reminder opens your chat with that friend in WhatsApp or your messages app, using the number you saved (or your share sheet, if you saved none). Tabs only opens the chat with the words filled in: nothing goes until you press send. Tabs keeps a copy of the last export and the last month's card in its private temporary folder until the next time the app starts, or until you leave the app once they're about an hour old (which can be days if you don't open Tabs). Delete everything deletes them too.

What leaves your phone — and why

1. Sharing a round with your crew (optional; needs your consent)

If you want a round to show up live on your friends' phones, you can share it. This only works while Share rounds with your crew and Processing outside South Africa are both switched on (Settings › Consent & permissions), and only when you tap "Share with crew" or join a friend's round with a code.

  • What's sent: the round's label, total and join code; each person's display name (yours, and the friends' names you typed), their share, who paid, who's in the round and who has squared up; your name when you join someone else's round; and an anonymous identifier for your phone. Tabs creates this identifier for you, with no email, password or phone number. Like any internet service, the server also sees your IP address when you connect. Our project's logs there can keep it for about a day, Supabase keeps its own records of connections to its service for a time its terms don't state, and Supabase's record of your sign-in keeps it for as long as your anonymous account exists.
  • What's never sent: your drinks, estimate, plan, body details, check-ins, safety contacts, friends' phone numbers or PayShap handles, or anything about how much you drank. A crew must never be able to see how drunk anyone got — that's a core design rule.
  • Who and where: our sync service is Supabase, provided to us by Supabase Pte. Ltd (Singapore), which processes the shared rounds on our behalf. The data is stored in Ireland, in the European Union (Amazon Web Services' eu-west-1 region). Because that's outside South Africa, it has its own consent switch (POPIA s72). To run its service, Supabase uses its own service providers, most of them in the United States (for example Amazon Web Services, Cloudflare and Google), and they may handle the data too. Supabase's data processing terms require it to bind each of them to terms that protect the data, in substance, as much as its own terms do, and it stays responsible for them. Supabase lists its current providers at https://supabase.com/legal/customer-resources/subprocessor-list. Each shared round is protected by row-level access rules, so only people in that round can see it. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS).
  • The protection in each country: in Ireland, the EU's data protection law (the GDPR) applies to the hosting. Singapore, where Supabase is based, has its own data protection law (the Personal Data Protection Act 2012). The United States has no general data protection law like POPIA, so data handled there is protected by Supabase's contracts with its providers, not by a law like POPIA.
  • Why (legal basis):
    • for your own information: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time;
    • for the names and amounts of the friends you add to a round you share: your and your crew's legitimate interest in keeping track of who owes what (POPIA s11(1)(f)). Only a display name, a share and the round's flags go for each person, never their contact details or anything about drinking. Supabase handles it under the terms of service and data processing terms our director accepted when he set the service up, before the company was registered (POPIA s72(1)(a)).
  • Withdrawing: switching off either of those two consents removes the rounds you've shared, your row on rounds you joined, and your anonymous cloud account from the server, and signs your phone out, so nothing is sent afterwards. If you have shared rounds, the app asks you to confirm first. Your rounds stay on your phone, and your friends keep their own copies on theirs.
  • Stopping one round: on a shared round's code sheet, Stop sharing this round deletes a round you shared from the server, or, on a round you joined, only your own row. The round stays on your phone.

About your friends' information. When you add a friend to a round, to your crew or as a safety contact, you're giving Tabs their name and, if you choose, their phone number and (for a crew friend) how to remind them and their PayShap number or handle. Friends' numbers and PayShap handles always stay on your phone. Their names leave it only if you share a round. Please only add people who'd be happy for you to, and please don't add anyone under 18.

If you're named in a round someone shared and don't want to be, email hello@krazybuilds.com with the round's label or join code and the name it used. We'll delete your row from our server, stop it being sent again, and let you know. Copies on your friends' phones are theirs to remove, and a deleted copy can stay in Supabase's backups until they expire (see "How long we keep things").

2. Buying Tabs+ (optional)

Tabs+ is sold through Google Play, and subscriptions are managed by RevenueCat on our behalf.

  • Google Play takes your payment under Google's own terms and privacy policy. Tabs never sees your card or bank details.
  • RevenueCat (in the United States) receives: an anonymous app-user identifier; the purchase details from Google Play (the product, price, currency, purchase and expiry dates, and the purchase token); basic device and app information (platform, OS version, app version, country and language); and your IP address.
  • When: only once you open the Tabs+ screen, buy, restore, or manage a subscription. After that, if you have subscribed, it's contacted when the app starts, so your Tabs+ is recognised. If you never open the Tabs+ screen, RevenueCat is never contacted.
  • Why (legal basis): when you open the Tabs+ screen, to show you the plans and prices you asked to see (steps you asked us to take before a purchase); if you buy, to provide the subscription (performing our contract with you). Both cover the transfer to the United States that this requires (POPIA s72(1)(c)).
  • The protection there: the United States has no general data protection law like POPIA, so your information there is protected by RevenueCat's own terms and security, not by a law like POPIA.

3. Pinning your home (optional)

If you type a home address, your phone's built-in maps service (Google's, on Android) looks it up once to find where it is on the map. If you pin your home with your current location, nothing is looked up. The saved home stays on your phone.

4. Getting a ride home (when you tap it)

Tapping a ride opens Uber or Bolt.

  • Uber: Tabs fills in the drop-off with your saved home (its pin, and the address or name you saved it under), if you have one, and asks Uber to set the pickup to where you are. Uber finds that itself: Tabs doesn't read your location for a ride.
  • Bolt: Tabs only opens it, and passes it nothing.
  • If neither app is on your phone, the same Uber link, or Bolt's website, opens in your browser instead.

From then on, those apps handle the trip under their own privacy policies.

5. Location

Tabs uses your location only when you ask:

  • a single reading to share where you are with a friend or a safety contact (for example when you ask someone to fetch you), to pin where you parked or move that pin, to see how far away your car is and which way, or to pin your home;
  • a live reading while the walk-back screen is open, to point you to your car.

It never tracks you in the background and keeps no history of where you've been. The parked-car pin stays on your phone. On the walk-back screen you can tap to open Google Maps (the app or its website) with your car's pin, for walking directions; from then on Google handles it under its own terms.

What Tabs does not do

  • No ads, and no advertising SDKs or advertising ID.
  • No usage analytics and no crash-reporting service in this version. RevenueCat's dashboard shows us subscription totals from the purchase records.
  • No selling or sharing of your data with data brokers or anyone else for marketing.
  • No account required — no email, no password.
  • No background tracking.
  • No money handling. Tabs never holds or moves money.
  • No "safe to drive" verdict. The estimate is an educational range with an always-visible disclaimer.
  • No AI processing of your data.

Your choices and rights (POPIA)

  • Consent is granular and can be withdrawn. In Settings › Consent & permissions there are three switches, and each does exactly what it says:
    • Notice a heavy stretch lets Tabs spot a sustained heavy stretch in your history, on your phone, and quietly offer support contacts.
    • Share rounds with your crew covers what's described above.
    • Processing outside South Africa covers the same. Switch anything off at any time and it takes effect straight away.
  • Access and correction: your data is on your phone, so you can see and edit it in the app (profile, sessions, rounds, contacts). To find out what we hold about you on a server (shared rounds and your anonymous cloud account at Supabase, subscription records at RevenueCat, or emails you've sent us), to get a copy, or to have it corrected, email hello@krazybuilds.com or write to our registered office. Our PAIA manual, at https://krazybuilds.com/paia/, explains how to make a request (POPIA sections 23 to 25; PAIA section 51).
  • Export: Settings › Export my data gives you a full copy as one file.
  • Deletion: Settings › Delete everything wipes everything on your phone and removes everything you shared from our server, including your anonymous cloud account. If you're offline at the time, Tabs finishes the server part the next time it's online. A deleted copy can stay in Supabase's encrypted backups until they expire; Supabase's public documents don't say how long that is on our plan. Deleting doesn't cancel a Tabs+ subscription — cancel that in Google Play. To have your purchase records deleted by RevenueCat, email us.

Security

On your phone, Tabs' data sits in the app's private storage. It's protected by Android's app sandbox and by Android's built-in storage encryption, which is tied to your screen lock. Tabs doesn't add its own separate encryption layer in this version. Shared-round data is protected in transit by TLS and on the server by per-round access rules, and Supabase encrypts the stored data and its backups. No system is perfectly secure, but we keep what leaves your phone to the minimum on purpose.

How long we keep things

  • On your phone: until you delete it (Delete everything) or remove the app.
  • Shared rounds (Supabase): until you stop sharing the round, switch off either sharing consent, or delete everything. A round someone else shared stays with them, but your own row on it is removed. Our server deletes a shared round, with everyone on it, 180 days after its last change, or 90 days after its last change once nobody on it owes anything.
  • Your anonymous cloud account (Supabase): until you switch off either sharing consent, or delete everything. If you remove Tabs without doing either, it stays on Supabase with no time limit, and so do the rounds you shared until they're deleted (above). Email us with the label or join code of a round you shared or joined, and we'll delete them.
  • When someone is taken off a shared round: when a friend leaves a round, or we take someone off it after they object, the server keeps a note of it by random ID only, never a name, so that the sharer's phone can't send them again. The note goes with the round.
  • Backups: a deleted copy of crew-sync data can stay in Supabase's encrypted backups until they expire. Supabase's public documents don't say how long that is on our plan.
  • Subscription records (RevenueCat / Google Play): for as long as needed to provide your subscription and meet tax and accounting law. Ask us to delete them.
  • Email you send us: as long as we need it to deal with your request, and to show that we did.

When you email us

Email to hello@krazybuilds.com is passed on by Cloudflare and kept in our mailbox, which Google runs. Both are United States companies, and Google may store email in the United States or other countries. Our mailbox runs under Google's standard consumer terms, not a business contract with us, and the United States has no general data protection law like POPIA. So please don't put health details, ID numbers or bank details in an email: we never need them to act on a request. We use your email only to answer you. If you'd rather not use email, write to us at our registered office (above).
